# How to update your website

You can change the words and pictures on your website yourself. You cannot break the design.

## Sign in
1. Go to **your website address followed by /admin** (for example `sequoiaassistedliving.com/admin`).
2. Click **Login with GitHub** and sign in. The first time, click **Authorize**.

## Change text
1. Click a section in the list, such as **3. Services**.
2. Click into any box and type your changes.
3. Click **Publish**, then **Publish now**.
4. Wait about one minute, then refresh your website.

Tips:
- Phone numbers, the email address and the street address are all in **9. Business Info**. Change them once and they update everywhere on the site, including the tap-to-call and map links.
- The big headline has two boxes: the first part, and the second part that shows in green italics.
- The Services and Why Choose Us lists have a fixed number of boxes. Edit the words inside them.

## Change a picture
1. Open **11. Logos & Images**.
2. Click **Choose different image**, then **Upload**, pick your file, then **Choose selected**.
3. Click **Publish** > **Publish now**.

Picture tips: use PNG files with a transparent background. The small top-left logo works best around 500 x 310 pixels. The large logo works best roughly square (about 620 x 640 pixels) with the artwork centered. Files under 5 MB.

## Made a mistake?
Edit the box again and publish again. If you are stuck, contact your web developer. They can restore any earlier version.
